Our Programs

Choose the right program for your child's age and development

🧸 Play Group

1.5 - 2.5 Years

A nurturing introduction to the world of learning through play, exploration, and social interaction.

  • Sensory Activities
  • Music & Movement
  • Story Time
  • Social Skills
  • Fine Motor Development

📚 Pre-KG

2.5 - 3.5 Years

Building foundational skills through structured activities, phonics, and creative expression.

  • Phonics Introduction
  • Number Awareness
  • Art & Craft
  • Language Development
  • Social & Emotional Growth

✏️ LKG

3.5 - 4.5 Years

Developing reading, writing, and numeracy skills through engaging, activity-based learning.

  • Reading & Writing
  • Basic Math Skills
  • Science Exploration
  • Creative Expression
  • Independence Building

🌟 UKG

4.5 - 5.5 Years

Preparing children for primary school with advanced literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking skills.

  • Advanced Phonics
  • Reading Comprehension
  • Math Problem Solving
  • Science & Nature
  • School Readiness

🌙 Day Care

Flexible Hours

Safe, nurturing, and home-like care for your little one while you're at work.

  • Safe & Secure Environment
  • Structured Activities
  • Nutritious Meals
  • Rest & Nap Time
  • After-School Care
