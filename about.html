Our Story

Our Story

BrightRoots Kids Care began its journey in 2024 as BrightRoots Academy, offering SpellBee International and Olympiad International coaching to help children strengthen their academic skills, confidence and competitive abilities.

Through our experience working closely with children, we came to understand something important — a child's learning journey begins much earlier than formal academics. The early years play a vital role in shaping a child's curiosity, communication, confidence, independence and approach towards learning.

This inspired us to take BrightRoots to the next level.

In 2026, BrightRoots Kids Care was established as a Preschool & Day Care in Tirupattur, with the aim of providing children with a strong foundation through meaningful early learning experiences.

We believe children in growing cities like Tirupattur deserve access to a well-structured, engaging and child-focused preschool experience. At BrightRoots, our goal is to combine quality early education with the warmth, care and individual attention every young child needs.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding everything we do — from curriculum to care

🔭

Our Vision

To build strong foundations in every child by nurturing curiosity, confidence, creativity and a lifelong love for learning.

We envision BrightRoots as a place where children feel happy to come to school, confident to express themselves and excited to discover something new every day.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a safe, caring and stimulating early-learning environment where every child has the opportunity to learn, explore and grow at their own pace.

  • ✓ Make learning meaningful through play, activities and hands-on experiences
  • ✓ Build strong foundations in communication, early literacy, numeracy and essential life skills
  • ✓ Encourage creativity, curiosity, confidence and independent thinking
  • ✓ Support the social and emotional development of every child
  • ✓ Provide individual attention and care while respecting each child's unique learning journey
  • ✓ Create a positive partnership between children, parents and educators
What Makes Us Different

What Makes BrightRoots Different?

Six pillars that set us apart

🌱

Strong Foundation, Not Just School Readiness

Our focus goes beyond preparing children for their next grade. We work towards developing communication, confidence, curiosity, independence, social skills and a positive attitude towards learning.

🎨

Learning Through Experience

Children learn best when they actively participate. Our approach combines play, stories, phonics, numeracy, creative activities, movement, exploration and age-appropriate hands-on experiences.

👩‍🏫

Qualified & Experienced Educators

Our children are guided by experienced educators with postgraduate qualifications who understand the importance of patience, encouragement and individual attention during the early years.

🧒

Individual Attention

Every child develops differently. We encourage children to learn and participate at their own pace while providing the guidance and support they need.

🛡️

Safe & Child-Friendly Environment

Our facilities include child-friendly classrooms, activity spaces, a play area, CCTV monitoring, security, a dedicated nap area and caring caregivers to provide children with a comfortable and supportive environment.

🏠

Preschool + Day Care

With preschool and day care under one roof, BrightRoots provides continuity of care and learning for children throughout their day.

Our Founder

Meet Our Founder

The vision behind BrightRoots Kids Care

✦ Founder & CEO

Janani G

Founder & CEO, BrightRoots Kids Care

BrightRoots was founded with a belief that quality education should begin with a strong foundation.

What started as BrightRoots Academy in 2024, supporting children through SpellBee International and Olympiad International coaching, evolved into a larger vision after experiencing first-hand how significantly the early years influence a child's future learning and development.

That vision led to the establishment of BrightRoots Kids Care Preschool & Day Care in 2026 — creating a space where children in Tirupattur can receive quality early education in an environment built around learning, exploration, care and confidence.
